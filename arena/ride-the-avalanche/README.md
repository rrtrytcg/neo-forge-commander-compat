# Ride the Avalanche lab

The card's native spell API is `Effect`. Without an `AILogic` case, `EffectAi` declines the generic effect, and `AI:RemoveDeck:All` also excludes it from ordinary AI selection. The `ride-the-avalanche` profile admits the card and casts only in our own main phase with an empty stack, a counter-receiving creature we control, and enough shared mana to pay both Ride and at least one other nonland, non-X, mana-only spell in hand. Native `CountersPutAi`, casting restrictions, payment, flash permission, and trigger resolution remain in force.

The opt-in hook is `RideTheAvalancheAi` at the top of `EffectAi.checkApiLogic`. The rule uses only public board state and cards in our hand; it has no policy randomness or cached plans. It builds Ride's scripted counter trigger to verify that our creature is actually a legal target, and holds when the shared mana check or useful creature check fails. The AI may still choose not to cast the follow-up after Ride resolves, so a qualifying Ride does not guarantee its trigger will fire.

The native baseline passed 2 checks; focused fixtures passed 17, covering normal selection, shared mana affordability, target availability including shroud, timing, opponent turns, native mana payment, a follow-up cast during combat, the native counter result, and profile isolation. The cumulative NeoForge 1.0.28 full gate passed 5,753 checks across 58 suites (17 new plus all 5,736 prior checks at exact counts). The installed-file guard checked 21,729 hashes with zero changes. The sealed candidate is `target/final-1.0.28-r4`, chained from `arena/selective-obliteration/target/final-1.0.28-r2`.

Paired two-player exposure games used identical custom decks, 120-second clocks, and seeds 130011 / 130012, profile off/on. Off had zero Ride casts, resolutions, or triggers on both seeds. On had 4/4 Ride cast/resolutions and 4 triggered counter resolutions on seed 130011; seed 130012 had 3/3 casts/resolutions, 2 triggers and 2 trigger resolutions. The third Ride on seed 130012 was not followed by another spell that turn. All four games exited cleanly without exceptions or clock limits. Card-use and trigger evidence only; no winner or strength claim. Logs, commands, witnesses, and hashes are in `verification-summary.json`.
